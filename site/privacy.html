Privacy policy & credits.

Yapper has no accounts, no servers of its own and no analytics. What you say and what it reads stay on your Mac, except the text you choose to send to a voice provider.

Last updated October 20, 2026

What Yapper collects

Nothing. Yapper doesn't collect, store or sell any personal data, and it sends no telemetry, usage statistics or crash reports. There's no Yapper account to sign up for.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your voice. Voice In turns speech into text on your Mac. The Loud Mouth animation also listens on your Mac to shape its mouth. Microphone audio is never uploaded or saved.
  • What was read and dictated. Spoken replies stay in a local history that's deleted on your schedule, from one hour to seven days. Yapper keeps only your most recent dictation, so you can correct it.
  • What Yapper learns. Your vocabulary and the corrections you make to dictated words are saved locally so it spells them right next time. To spell names correctly, it may read words from the window you're dictating into. Those are used once, then discarded.
  • Your API keys. They're stored in the macOS Keychain and sent only to the provider they belong to.

What leaves your Mac

  • Text for a voice provider, if you pick one. With ElevenLabs or OpenAI, the text being read aloud goes straight from your Mac to that provider, on your own API key. It's handled under their privacy policy: ElevenLabs, OpenAI. With the Mac's built-in voice, nothing leaves.
  • One-time model downloads. Speech and formatting models are downloaded from Hugging Face the first time you use them. After that they run offline.
  • Update checks. Yapper checks GitHub for new versions and downloads updates from it. Like any website, GitHub sees your IP address when this happens. Updates are checked against Yapper's signing key before they install.
  • Text you dictate. Voice In types the text into the app you're using. From there, that app handles it under its own policies.

Permissions

Accessibility lets Yapper find the latest reply and type what you dictate. Microphone is only needed if you turn on Voice In. You can revoke either one any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It's hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it serves them.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Every change is also visible in the public source code. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
