048the right ⌥ contract

One key. Six moves.

The right Option key — the one nothing else wants. No two-hand chords, no collisions with app shortcuts. Rather use fn, Right ⌃ or Right ⌘? Swap it in Settings.

Right ⌥Read latest — or pause / resume whatever's playing
Right ⌥×2Force-read the focused chat, superseding current audio
Right ⌥holdTalk back — speak, let go, and it's typed into the chatNew
Right ⌥+SRead selection — any highlighted text, any app
Right ⌥+⏎Conversation Mode on / offBeta
Right ⌥+→Skip to the next queued reply

⌥-click, ⌥-drag, the em-dash you type every day — it's still a normal Option key. Anything Yapper doesn't claim passes straight through.

061the fine print

Recorded locally.

No cloud middleman. API keys live in the macOS Keychain. With a voice provider, the only thing that leaves the machine is the cleaned text, sent straight to ElevenLabs or OpenAI on your key. With the built-in voice, nothing leaves at all. No relay server, no accounts, no telemetry.

Your voice stays here. Voice In runs Whisper on your Mac. Your audio is never uploaded, and nothing is sent anywhere until you press Return yourself.

Your words expire. Spoken text exists only in local history — purged on your schedule, one hour to seven days, enforced hourly. The only dictation kept is your last one, so you can correct it — and it never leaves the Mac.

Clipboard sacred. Yapper borrows the clipboard to grab a reply or type what you said, then restores it byte-for-byte — images and files included.

Permissions, asked once. Accessibility, so Yapper can see the chat and type into it — the first-run guide walks you through it. Microphone only if you switch on Voice In.

Signed, sealed, self-updating. Every build is signed and notarized by Apple. Updates arrive in the app and are checked against Yapper's signing key before they install.

082q & a

Before you press play.

Do I need an ElevenLabs account?

No. Out of the box Yapper talks with the Mac's built-in voice — free, offline, no sign-up. When you want a voice that sounds like a person, paste an ElevenLabs or OpenAI API key into Settings. Yapper checks the key works before it goes into your Keychain.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Yapper is free and open source. If you add an ElevenLabs or OpenAI key, they bill you directly for what you use — Yapper takes no cut and has no subscription.

Which apps can it read?

The Claude and Codex desktop apps, plus Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Dia, Edge, Firefox and Brave. Other chat apps — the ChatGPT desktop app included — work on a best-effort basis. For everything else there's Read Selection: highlight any text and press Right ⌥ + S.

Does my voice leave my Mac?

No. Voice In transcribes with Whisper on your Mac. The only download is the speech model itself, once — about 80 MB to 1.5 GB, depending on which one you pick. After that it works offline.

What permissions does it need?

Accessibility, so it can find the latest reply and type what you dictate. Microphone, only if you switch on Voice In. That's it.

Will it run on my Mac?

macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon or Intel — it's one universal download. Voice In's most accurate model wants Apple silicon; Intel Macs start on a lighter one.

Is there a Windows version?

A port is being built in the same repo. No date yet — watch the GitHub repo if you want to know when it lands.
