what's on the tape

Three moves. That's the manual.

Right ⌥tap

Read latest

One tap pulls the newest reply out of the focused chat and starts talking. Tap again to pause, again to resume. Double-tap to force-read whatever chat is in front — it supersedes anything already playing.

works while you make coffee
Right ⌥+S

Read selection

Highlight anything, anywhere — an email, a doc, a 40-page changelog — and it's audio. Works in any app that can select text, browsers and terminals included.

every article is a podcast now
Right ⌥+⏎

Conversation mode

Press REC and every new AI reply auto-plays the moment it finishes generating. Replies queue in order and never talk over each other; skip ahead with Right ⌥ + →. Toggle off, the queue clears.

the right ⌥ contract

One key. Five moves.

The right Option key — the one nothing else wants. No two-hand chords, no collisions with app shortcuts.

Right ⌥Read latest — or pause / resume whatever's playing
Right ⌥×2Force-read the focused chat, superseding current audio
Right ⌥+SRead selection — any highlighted text, any app
Right ⌥+⏎Conversation Mode on / off
Right ⌥+→Skip to the next queued reply

⌥-click, ⌥-drag, the em-dash you type every day — it's still a normal Option key. Anything Yapper doesn't claim passes straight through.

the fine print

Recorded locally.

No cloud middleman. Your ElevenLabs key lives in the macOS Keychain. The only thing that ever leaves the machine is the cleaned text, sent straight to ElevenLabs with your key. No relay server, no accounts, no telemetry.

Your words expire. Spoken text exists only in local history — purged on your schedule, one hour to seven days, enforced hourly. No other transcript ever touches the disk.

Clipboard sacred. Conversation Mode captures a reply by pressing the app's own Copy button, then restores your clipboard byte-for-byte — images and files included.

No truncated tapes. Audio is published to the cache only when complete. A stopped or failed read can never come back to haunt a replay.

One permission, once. Yapper asks for macOS Accessibility so it can see the chat on your screen — granted once, live immediately, no relaunch.

41 tests stand guard. The markdown cleaner, sentence segmentation, the conversation state machine, and history retention are all pinned down by a logic suite that runs on every build.
